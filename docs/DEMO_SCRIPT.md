# Demo Script (Target: 2 minutes 30 seconds)

The hackathon rules require a video shorter than three minutes. Record a screen capture that shows the real app working, not only slides.

## 0:00–0:15 — Problem
“Freight buyers often receive quotes with inconsistent fee details, limited price context, and uncertainty about which offer deserves closer review. FreightPilot AI connects quote analysis to an explicit PayPal payment workflow.”

## 0:15–0:40 — Shipment and quote comparison
- Open a seeded shipment.
- Point to cargo/route details and the rate estimate.
- Compare three quotes by total, transit time, and terms.
- State clearly if the data is synthetic/demo data.

## 0:40–1:10 — AI and explainable risk
- Open a quote with missing fee details or an outlier price.
- Show the exact risk flag and evidence.
- Ask the Freight Copilot to compare quotes.
- Emphasize that the AI explains evidence and does not verify a carrier or authorize payment.

## 1:10–2:05 — PayPal Sandbox
- Select a quote.
- Show checkout amount/currency and Sandbox label.
- Launch PayPal checkout, approve using a Sandbox buyer, and return to the app.
- Show the backend-confirmed capture/payment state, not just a success redirect.

## 2:05–2:30 — Impact and limitations
“This prototype shows how AI-assisted quote review can lead to a traceable payment action. The payment is in PayPal Sandbox, and the quote/forecast data is demonstrative. A real launch would require validated data and PayPal eligibility/compliance review.”

## Recording checklist
- Hide local secrets and browser developer tools containing sensitive environment values.
- Use synthetic names and amounts.
- Make the browser text readable at video size.
- Record in English.
- Keep the video publicly viewable on YouTube and under three minutes, as required by the official rules.
